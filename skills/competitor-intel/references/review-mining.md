# Review mining playbook (public reactions)

Adapted from customer-research (Mode 2) in coreyhaines31/marketingskills and competitive-teardown in alirezarezvani/claude-skills.

## Where to look 
| Source | Search pattern | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| G2 / Capterra / Software Advice / GetApp | `"<name>" reviews site:capterra.com` (also capterra.in) | Pros and cons, support and pricing complaints |
| Google Play / App Store | `"<name>" app reviews`; 1–3 star reviews first | Mobile bugs, sync, login problems |
| Trustpilot | `site:trustpilot.com <name>` | Billing, contract and cancellation complaints |
| Reddit | `site:reddit.com <name>` in the subreddits where your buyers talk (role, industry and country subs) | Unfiltered language |
| YouTube | demo and review comments | Setup pain, real usage |
| LinkedIn / Product Hunt | launch posts and their comments | Positioning, early user reactions |
| Vendor site | pricing and feature pages, changelog | Features, pricing anchors |

Read the 4-star reviews closely: they name the one thing missing.
Capterra, G2 and some vendor sites may block fetches from the cloud. If so, use search snippets, mark the rows `unverified`, and list the blocked domains in the final report.

## What to extract per item
Source URL and date · verbatim quote · sentiment · theme tag (pain / praise / switching trigger / missing feature / pricing) · reviewer profile (role or company type, size, country).

## Synthesis
Rank themes by frequency × intensity. The most common complaint across a tier becomes a stand_out takeaway.
